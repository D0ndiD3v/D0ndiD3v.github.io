My main idea for this simulator, was a deeper rooted need to observe generations pass, genes change, and to be able to actually witness a variety of lifetimes in a manageable time frame. My instinct told me to look at it like I'm making a terrarium for an ant colony in my room, just preparing the habitat, gathering the species and watching them live.

## Where this started

What caught my attention before even trying to make a thing of my own were 'The Bibites', a game of a simmilar genre, in which I've spent many hours. The idea of following certain creatures, watching them evolve, thrive and inevitably die or get outpaced by others really stuck to me. Though it has been years between me playing the game and starting to make my own thing with my own rules, but future posts will certainly expand on that in a more meaningful way.

<span class="margin-note">One of the first thoughts I have regarding this project was 'why do I have to play by someone elses rules?' and that's what gave me the first push to make something of my own.</span>

## What I actually know going in

The idea was feeling impossible at first. How would someone with basic understanding of coding even attempt to make a simulator like this? That was my first thought, as I only taken some basic classes of C++ during my time in Highschool, and learned a little HTML/CSS besides that, nothing too fancy, just the basics. But that isn't enough to stop me, I've dabbled in Python somewhat in my free time, which didn't necessarily make me feel confident, but it gave me enough hope to go out there and learn things on the way. I often reassure myself that while I don't have the necessary knowledge yet, I can learn it by trying things, looking for ideas and implementing them throughout.

## The idea, roughly

The rough sketch for this project is a simulator of species evolving through time. My goal is to make a simple toy, to track how species evolve and adapt to new conditions, how different traits emerge and dissapear through generations, and simply to have something to look at in the background just to come back every once in a while just to check on your little zoo.

## Why post about it at all

The main reason for starting this blog page was to aid me with keeping track of my progress and the whole development process and noting down anything important or interesting. Having such a blog also makes it easy to share the whole thing with others, and who knows, maybe people will find interest in running their own virtual terrarium and would also like to read about what's happening behind the scenes.

## What's next

For now my main goal will be expanding on the blog page and the spider simulator. I have a rough estimate about where I want this to go, so now it's simply a matter of putting it all together, while the picture is getting clearer with each day.
